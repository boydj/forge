# Issues

`/~user/repo/issues/` lists open issues, links to the closed ones, and
has *open a new issue*.

- **Open**: follow *open a new issue* and upload your text. The first line
  is the title, the rest is the body.
- **Comment**: on the issue page, follow *comment* and upload the text.
- **Edit**: the *edit* links on an issue or a comment. Lagrange's *Edit
  page with Titan* loads the current text for you.
- **Close or reopen**: follow the link and type `close` or `reopen` at the
  prompt, optionally followed by a comment.

Anyone who can read the repository can open issues and comment. The
author and the repository's writers can edit and close.

Follow a repository's issues at `/~user/repo/issues/feed`.
